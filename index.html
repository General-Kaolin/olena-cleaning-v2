✨ Regular Maintenance Cleaning For homes cleaned regularly

Note: Regular cleaning is for homes that are regularly maintained and do not require significant dirt removal.

Kitchen
  • Wipe countertops & accessible surfaces
  • Wash sink & faucet
  • Wipe cooktop
  • Wipe exterior of kitchen appliances
  • Wipe cabinet fronts
  • Wash microwave inside & out
  • Vacuum & mop floors
Bathrooms
  • Clean & disinfect toilets
  • Clean sinks & faucets
  • Clean bathtub and/or shower
  • Clean shower doors & glass
  • Clean mirrors & accessible surfaces
  • Vacuum & mop floors
  • Take out trash
Bedrooms & Living Rooms
  • Dust accessible surfaces
  • Make beds as needed
  • Vacuum carpets & rugs
  • Vacuum & mop hard floors
  • Wipe visible fingerprints & stains
  • Remove cobwebs
  • Clean doorknobs & light switches
🌿 General / R&B Cleaning Includes everything in Regular + detailed focus

Kitchen & Bathrooms
  • Clean countertops & backsplash
  • Thoroughly clean cooktop, range hood & exterior
  • Clean accessible areas around appliances
  • Thoroughly clean & disinfect toilets, tubs & showers

  • Clean baseboards in bathrooms
Living Areas & Extras
  • Dust furniture, shelves & light fixtures
  • Vacuum doors & door frames

  • Thoroughly clean countertops & backsplash

  • Clean inside cabinets (if empty)
  • Clean range hood & available filters

Bathrooms & Details
  • Remove soap scum where possible

  • Clean baseboards, corners & hard-to-reach areas
  • Thoroughly disinfect all fixtures & tile
Around the House
  • Clean windows from the inside & window sills
  • Clean doors & door frames
  • Dust ventilation vents & light fixtures
  • Vacuum under accessible furniture
  • Special attention to accumulated dust & grime
